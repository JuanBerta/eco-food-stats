How to read this

Nutrients is carbs + fat + protein + vitamins. Density is nutrients per 1,000 calories — high-density foods fill your nutrient bars without filling your stomach. Balance is the weakest nutrient divided by the strongest (100 = perfectly even).

Skill-point gain in Eco comes from your whole stomach, not from one food: the average nutrient level, how balanced the four nutrients are, variety, tastiness and cravings all play in. Use density and balance to pick foods that complement each other. Tastiness is randomized per player, so it isn't listed.

Labor, craft time and ingredient amounts are base values before skill talents, upgrade modules and table bonuses.
